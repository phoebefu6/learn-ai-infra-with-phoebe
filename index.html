Learn AI Infra with Phoebe

The platform behind your whole data team

A data platform is eight layers, five personas and a lot of money. This course builds the reference architecture that lets a data engineer ingest and clean, an analyst build dashboards and reports, a data scientist train and deploy, an AI engineer ship LLM applications, and a business client ask a question and trust the answer - all on one stack. Vendor-neutral, with real options and honest cost bands at every layer, every price fetched from the vendor's own billing feed with the date attached. A live simulator computes the bill as you build: watch it climb from $0 to $11,295 a month as your five people come unblocked, then flip one lever and watch analytics egress add $21,402 a year for exactly the same work. And the sequencing rule the whole course exists to teach: self-serve is layer eight, and buying it first is the most expensive mistake in enterprise data.
